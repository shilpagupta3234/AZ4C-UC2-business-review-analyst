# Saffron Trail Foods: Finance Calendar

## Months
Reports use calendar months, from the 1st to the last day of the month.

## Financial year
Our financial year runs from **1 April to 31 March** and is named after the calendar year in which it ends.

- **FY26** = 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026
- **FY27** = 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027

## Quarters
| Quarter | Months |
| --- | --- |
| Q1 | April, May, June |
| Q2 | July, August, September |
| Q3 | October, November, December |
| Q4 | January, February, March |

So **Q2 FY27** is July to September 2026, and **Q1 FY27** is April to June 2026.

## Data available
Sales data covers April 2025 to September 2026. There is no data for October 2026 onwards yet.
